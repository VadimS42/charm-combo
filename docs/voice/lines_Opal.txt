OPAL (Slime, formerly of a jar)

Voice: Opal, slime. An adult who spent thirty-one years hiding in a jar in the woods north of Duskhaven, listening to people on the path. Soft, polite, a little formal; stutters on the first word when she's nervous, apologises and then corrects herself. Very exact about her collection (counts, where each thing came from). Gets braver and funnier as the story goes on. Shy, never childish.
Say it: Opal = OH-pul · Ambrose = AM-brohz · Coombs = KOOMZ

80 lines. Emotion = the portrait she shows; after it, how to deliver the line.
