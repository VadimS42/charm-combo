HAZEL MOSSGROVE (Dryad of the old hazel)

Voice: Hazel Mossgrove, dryad, bound to an old hazel tree for about two hundred years. Slow, calm, warm, few words. Her threats are polite and her jokes are bone-dry; she rarely raises her voice. Lonely underneath and not used to company. Jealousy comes out as calm, playful menace.
