AMARA DUSKROSE (Succubus, summoned by mistake)

Voice: Amara Duskrose, succubus. Ancient, unhurried, precise. Low, dry, faintly old-fashioned; she finds sloppiness more offensive than rudeness. Flirting is slow and confident, never giggly. When she's actually hurt or scared she goes quiet and plain, not loud.
Say it: Amara = ah-MAH-ruh · Duskrose = DUSK-rose · Duskhaven = DUSK-hay-vun · grimoire = grim-WAHR

83 lines. Emotion = the portrait she shows; after it, how to deliver the line.
