@@ ui-0008
Swap and match

@@ ui-0009
Swap two gems next to each other to line up three or more of the same kind. Matched gems pop and score points; new ones fall in from the top.

@@ ui-0010
Reach her goal

@@ ui-0011
Each chapter has a points goal and a number of moves. Reach the goal before your moves run out and the story carries on. There's no timer: take as long as you like.

@@ ui-0012
What she likes

@@ ui-0013
Every girl has a favourite gem and one she dislikes (it's on her card in the roster). Matching her favourite fills the Charm ring faster.

@@ ui-0014
Charm Burst

@@ ui-0015
Every match fills the Charm ring. When it's full you get a Charm Burst: +150 points.

@@ ui-0016
Special gems

@@ ui-0017
Match 4 in a row to make a Rocket, which clears its row or column. Match 5, or an L or T shape, to make a Starburst. Swap a special gem to set it off; swap two together for a Mega Blast.

@@ ui-0018
Combos

@@ ui-0019
When falling gems make new matches on their own, that's a combo. Each link in the chain scores more than the last.

@@ ui-0020
Cursed shards

@@ ui-0021
Dark, cracked shards cost you points when they're part of a match. The Hammer smashes one for free.

@@ ui-0022
Items

@@ ui-0023
+3 Moves, the Hammer (smash any one gem) and Shuffle (new board) sit under the board. Buy them, and one-chapter boosts, in the Item Shop with the stars you earn.

@@ ui-0024
Your replies

@@ ui-0025
After each chapter you pick a reply. A warm one starts her next chapter with bonus points; a teasing one costs a few, but she likes those too.

@@ ui-0026
Out of moves

@@ ui-0027
Running out still pays you a few stars. Try again, use +3 Moves, or visit the shop. Lose three times in a row and you can carry on with the story anyway.

@@ ui-0028
Dialogue controls

@@ ui-0029
Click, Space or Enter: next line. Hold Ctrl or press Skip: skip lines you've read. A or Auto: lines play on by themselves. L, Log or scroll up: read back. H: hide the text on full-screen pictures. Text speed is in Settings.
