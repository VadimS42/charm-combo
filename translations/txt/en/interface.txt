@@ ui-0008
Swap and match

@@ ui-0009
Swap two gems next to each other to line up three or more of the same kind. Matched gems pop and score points; new ones fall in from the top.

@@ ui-0010
Reach her goal

@@ ui-0011
Each chapter has a goal and a number of moves. At first it's points; from her third chapter it's something of hers to do on the board, and in her last both. Reach it before your moves run out and the story carries on. There's no timer: take as long as you like.

@@ ui-0012
What she likes

@@ ui-0013
Every girl has a favourite gem and one she dislikes (on her card in the roster, and at the top of her side in a chapter). Her favourite scores double and fills the Charm ring faster; the one she dislikes scores half.

@@ ui-0014
Her gift

@@ ui-0015
Once you've cleared her first chapter, her card on the roster has a gift for her. Give it and her favourite gem shows up more often in all her chapters, for good.

@@ ui-0016
Charm Burst

@@ ui-0017
Every match fills the Charm ring. In a girl's first chapter a full ring is a Charm Burst: +150 points. From her second chapter it's one use of her own power instead: its button lights up next to the ring, use it when you like. The ring fills again after.

@@ ui-0018
Special gems

@@ ui-0019
Match 4 in a row to make a Rocket, which clears its row or column. Match 5, or an L or T shape, to make a Starburst. Swap a special gem to set it off; swap two together for a Mega Blast.

@@ ui-0020
Combos

@@ ui-0021
When falling gems make new matches on their own, that's a combo. Each link in the chain scores more than the last.

@@ ui-0022
Cursed shards

@@ ui-0023
Dark, cracked shards cost you points when they're part of a match. The Hammer smashes one for free.

@@ ui-0024
Items

@@ ui-0025
The Time Scroll (three more moves), the Hammer (smash any one gem) and the Shuffle Wand (a new board) sit under the board. Buy them, and potions that help a whole chapter, in the Item Shop with the stars you earn.

@@ ui-0026
Hard: things happen on your date

@@ ui-0027
Every few moves something happens around you: the panel on her side says what and when. It might raise what it takes to win her over, turn gems into cursed shards, cost you a move or halve your Charm ring. Matching her favourite gem holds it off a move; a Charm Burst clears it.

@@ ui-0028
Amara: sealed pages

@@ ui-0029
Some gems carry her ink seal. A sealed gem can't be picked up, but it still counts in a match you make by moving the gems beside it. The first match, blast or Hammer breaks the seal and the gem stays.

@@ ui-0030
Amara: break every seal

@@ ui-0031
To find the book, break every seal on the board. The counter under the board shows how many are done.

@@ ui-0032
Amara's power: Correct Lines

@@ ui-0033
Press her power button (or 4), then pick a gem: its whole row and column clear. Seals in them break.

@@ ui-0034
Marina: the tide

@@ ui-0035
In her chapters everything floats: gems rise, and new ones come up from the bottom.

@@ ui-0036
Marina: the pearl comb

@@ ui-0037
Pieces of Gran's comb start near the bottom. They can't be matched or moved: clear the gems above them and they rise. One that reaches the top row is yours.

@@ ui-0038
Marina's power: High Tide

@@ ui-0039
Press her power button (or 4): the two rows nearest the surface clear, and everything rises two rows.

@@ ui-0040
Opal: moss

@@ ui-0041
Moss grows under some cells. A match, blast or Hammer on the cell clears it, and some moss hides one of her lost things.

@@ ui-0042
Opal: her lost collection

@@ ui-0043
Clear the moss to find her things: buttons, marbles, keys, the glass eye. Find enough of them to win.

@@ ui-0044
Opal's power: Absorb

@@ ui-0045
Press her power button (or 4), then pick a gem: every gem of that colour is cleared, and the moss under them.

@@ ui-0046
Hazel: seeds

@@ ui-0047
Seeds sit on the board and fall like gems, but can't be moved or matched. Every match right next to one grows it: seed, sprout, blossom. A blossom pops: +100 and the cells around it clear. Ember matches don't count: she dislikes ember.

@@ ui-0048
Hazel: grow blossoms

@@ ui-0049
Grow enough seeds into blossoms to win. A new seed drops in every few moves.

@@ ui-0050
Hazel's power: Summer

@@ ui-0051
Press her power button (or 4): every seed and sprout on the board grows one stage.

@@ ui-0052
Your replies

@@ ui-0053
After each chapter you pick a reply. A warm one starts her next chapter with bonus points (two extra moves when the chapter is only her goal); a teasing one costs a few points, but she likes those too.

@@ ui-0054
Out of moves

@@ ui-0055
Running out still pays you a few stars. Try again, read a Time Scroll, or visit the shop. Later chapters are hard without power-ups: short on stars, replay a chapter you've cleared from her card on the roster. Lose three times in a row and you can carry on with the story anyway.

@@ ui-0056
Dialogue controls

@@ ui-0057
Click, Space or Enter: next line. Hold Ctrl or press Skip: skip lines you've read. A or Auto: lines play on by themselves. L, Log or scroll up: read back. H: hide the text on full-screen pictures. Text speed is in Settings.
